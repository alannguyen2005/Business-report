Q1 2025 (Jan-Mar) had a structural revenue trough averaging $263-269K/day — 35% below the $408K/day baseline — causing the entire -8.3% YoY decline for 2025. April 2025 recovered instantly, ruling out a permanent demand shift.

**Why:** The Q1 2025 trough affected all 5 stores and all 5 categories at identical rates (-8.1% to -8.8%), pointing to a demand-wide or operational event rather than a product/regional cause. Root cause not yet identified — flagged for diagnostic phase.

**How to apply:** If continuing analysis on this dataset, the diagnostic phase should focus exclusively on what external or operational event caused the Q1 2025 demand shock. Do not investigate store or category factors — they were all equally affected.

Key metrics:
- Total actuals revenue (Jan 2023 - May 2026): $522.4M
- 2025 full-year YoY: -8.27% (vs 2024)
- 2026 Jan-May partial YoY: +23.78% recovery
- Holiday uplift: +37.2% (strongest contextual driver)
- Weekend premium: +30% vs weekdays
- Promotion lift: only +3.1% (consistent across all segments, not a paradox)
- Store_West is a persistent structural laggard ($10.7K/day avg vs $16.8K network avg)
- No Simpson's Paradox detected in promotion lift analysis
