March 2026 -54% revenue drop is a confirmed recurring seasonal trough (not structural): March 2025 also dropped -51% vs adjacent months with identical V-shape recovery in April both years.

**Why:** B2B Enterprise/Platform orders collapse in March (budget freeze / fiscal year transition); Enterprise fell -86% Feb→Mar contributing 67.9% of the drop; SMB grew +21% in March, partially masking severity.

**How to apply:** When presenting March numbers, always compare to March prior year, not prior month. Flag seasonality in the narrative so stakeholders do not misread as a structural problem.

Q1 2026 grew +41.8% QoQ (genuine volume-driven, not discount-induced):
- 68.9% of growth from more orders (66 → 85 orders)
- Mid-Market contributed 48.1% of revenue gain (+74.1% QoQ)
- North region contributed 85.8% of growth (+152.6% QoQ)
- Platform product generated 86.0% of growth (+71.8% QoQ)
- Discount rate barely changed (0.136 → 0.140); churn improved dramatically (0.381 → 0.159)
