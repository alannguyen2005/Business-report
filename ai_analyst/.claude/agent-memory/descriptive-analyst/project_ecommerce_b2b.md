Confirmed figures (descriptive run 2026-05-20): Q1 2026 revenue $131,360 (+41.8% QoQ from $92,669; +15.9% YoY from $113,379). March 2026: $25,002 (-54.1% vs Feb 2026 $54,432; +12.2% vs Mar 2025 $22,292).

**Why:** March trough is a structural seasonal pattern — identical -54% MoM drop in both Mar 2025 and Mar 2026. Volume loss (11 fewer orders vs Feb) explains 66% of March decline; AOV contraction explains 34%. Discount rate had near-zero correlation with revenue (r=0.099).

**How to apply:** When analyzing Q3 2026 or future quarters from this dataset, always model the third-month trough explicitly. Do not treat March-type drops as anomalies — they are structural. April rebounds sharply in both years (+89% in 2025, +132% in 2026). Q3 2025 was the second-lowest quarter ($100,191) — Q3 2026 may follow a similar soft pattern.

Key confirmed patterns for future sessions:
- All three segments (Enterprise +26%, Mid-Market +74%, SMB +33%) grew QoQ in Q1 2026 — no segment was a drag
- North region drove ~48% of the $38.7K QoQ gain, jumping from 23.5% to 39.0% share — geographic concentration risk
- East (-10.4%) and West (-11.6%) contracted QoQ despite strong aggregate — partial Simpson's paradox confirmed
- Platform product at 60.6% of Q1 revenue (+72% QoQ); Support declined -29.7% — product concentration risk
- Discount-revenue correlation is r=0.099 — pricing hypothesis rejected as March cause
- Mid-Market fastest growing (+74.1% QoQ) but also most volatile within-quarter (only 6 orders in March)
- Q4 2025 was the cyclical trough; Q1 2026 reversed 3 consecutive quarters of decline
